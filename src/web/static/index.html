A campaign moves through six stages. Every number on the other side of this is measured or computed, never assumed: channels that fail the live gate are dropped before they can reach the table.

  1. DiscoverySeed channels expand through forwards, mentions and a curated B2B seed set.
  2. Live gatePublic web preview must answer, hold ≥1,000 subscribers, and post within 14 days.
  3. RelevanceRecent posts scored with BM25 plus dense embeddings, fused by reciprocal rank.
  4. TriagePersona fit and brand safety decide whether a channel may spend your budget.
  5. PricingCPM ceiling from CTR × CVR × CAC, target bid discounted by data confidence.
  6. DeliverablesEight-sheet RTL Excel workbook and a flat UTF-8 CSV.
